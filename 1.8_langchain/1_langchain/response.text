**Jesus Christ** is the central figure of **Christianity** and one of the most influential figures in human history. 

Depending on whether he is viewed from a historical, Christian, or other religious perspective, his identity carries different dimensions:

---

### 1. The Christian Perspective
For the over 2.4 billion Christians worldwide, Jesus is much more than a historical figure:
* **The Messiah / Christ:** "Christ" is not his last name; it comes from the Greek word *Christos*, meaning "Anointed One" (the Jewish *Messiah* promised in the Old Testament).
* **The Son of God:** Christians believe Jesus is the Son of God and the second person of the **Holy Trinity** (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit)—fully God and fully human.
* **The Savior:** Christian theology teaches that Jesus lived a sinless life, died on a Roman cross as a sacrifice for the sins of humanity, and was **resurrected from the dead** three days later. His resurrection offers believers forgiveness, salvation, and eternal life.
* **His Teachings:** He emphasized loving God, loving one's neighbor, forgiving enemies, caring for the poor, and living with humility.

---

### 2. The Historical Perspective
Mainstream historians (both secular and religious) generally agree on the following historical facts about Jesus:
* **Life and Place:** He was a 1st-century Jewish teacher and preacher born around 4–6 BCE in Bethlehem and raised in Nazareth (in modern-day Israel/Palestine).
* **Ministry:** Around age 30, he began a public ministry in Judea and Galilee, gathering followers (disciples), teaching about the "Kingdom of God," and gaining a reputation as a healer.
* **Execution:** Around 30–33 CE, he was arrested in Jerusalem, tried, and executed by crucifixion under the authority of the Roman governor, **Pontius Pilate**.

---

### 3. Views in Other Religions
* **Islam:** Jesus (known in Arabic as *Isa*) is revered as one of God’s most important prophets and messengers. Muslims believe in his virgin birth, his miracles, and his eventual return, but they do **not** believe he is the Son of God, nor do they believe he was crucified (Islam teaches he was raised directly to heaven by God).
* **Judaism:** Jewish tradition views Jesus as a historical figure and a Jewish teacher of his time, but does not accept him as the divine Son of God or the promised Messiah.

---

### 4. Global Impact
Jesus’s life and teachings profoundly shaped world history:
* **The Calendar:** The modern global calendar (BC/AD or BCE/CE) was originally structured around his estimated birth date.
* **Culture and Ethics:** His ethical teachings have deeply influenced concepts of human rights, charity, law, and morality across the globe.
* **Art and Literature:** He has been the subject of more artwork, music, literature, and scholarly study than virtually any other individual in history.
